**Module 4. Your goal:** Create two finish variations from the original reference, compare them against your brief, and keep the glossy base for the guided video.

## Before you start

- Complete [Module 3](../build-your-first-workflow/) and reopen your saved image workflow.
- Have the product brief and reference image you prepared earlier.
- Keep using **Seedream 5.5** so the model and workflow stay familiar. Select the exact UI option `seedream 5.5`.

You will keep the graph and change its instructions. This is how a reusable workflow adapts to a new creative task.

If you reopen the included Seedream template instead, it still defaults to **Pro**. Override its model with `seedream 5.5`, replace the reference and use the full course prompt. If the option is missing, update the BytePlus pack and restart ComfyUI.

## Your workflow on the canvas

Reuse the **same three-node graph** from Module 3: **Load Image -> BytePlus Seedream -> Save Image**. You do not need extra nodes or a separate branch for each variation.

In **BytePlus Seedream**, change only the final finish sentence between A and B. In **Save Image**, change `filename_prefix` to `Course/variation-A` or `Course/variation-B` so you can identify the outputs. Keep the original reference, model and generation settings the same. **Save Image** shows the new result after each run.

## Prepare the reference and brief

<Steps>

1. Upload the original `byteplus_logo.png` in **Load Image**, or your original product reference. Keep its connection to Seedream's `image_1` input. The base, variation A and variation B all use this original reference; do not feed a generated badge back into Seedream.
2. Compare finishes: glossy enamel versus matte ceramic. Keep the full logo, placement, background and lighting instructions the same. Use your brief to judge which finish fits better.
3. Keep `size_preset` at `(2K) 2048x2048 (1:1)`, `output_format` at `png`, `background` at `opaque`, `generation_count` at `1` (one image), and `prompt_optimization` at `standard`.
4. Find **control after generate** and choose `fixed`. If your frontend shows this row twice, set both rows to `fixed`. Keep the same seed number, reference and model for the base and both variations.

</Steps>

Use the [course settings table in Module 2](../set-up-api-key/#generate-your-first-image) to check the values. Keep them and the original reference unchanged across the comparison.

<Shot src="tutorials/byteplus-image-settings.webp" alt="Seedream 5.5 settings with seed 42, both control after generate rows fixed, square 2K size, standard optimization, PNG, opaque background and one image" caption="Keep these controls unchanged for A and B; fixed is a local rerun setting, not a guarantee of matching results." />

For **Seedream 5.5**, the seed widget is a **local rerun control**. Its value is **not sent** to the image API. `fixed` stops ComfyUI changing that local number after a run; changing it can make ComfyUI rerun the node instead of reusing its cache. It is **not a deterministic** A/B guarantee. Even with the same number and original reference, results can differ beyond the finish instruction you changed. Each new generation can use your account balance.

## Create variation A

Use the same full base prompt and append the glossy enamel finish sentence:

```text
Turn the supplied BytePlus logo into a glossy 3D badge on a clean white studio background with soft shadows. Preserve the exact BytePlus symbol, the word BytePlus, and their proportions. Use BytePlus blue (#0066FC) for the symbol and dark charcoal lettering. Mount the full logo on one rounded white badge, centered with generous empty space. Front-facing product photography, soft diffused lighting, crisp edges. No other text, no extra logos, no decorative props. Give the badge a glossy enamel finish.
```

Before running, set Save Image's `filename_prefix` to `Course/variation-A`. Press **Run**, review the output and copy its file from `output/Course` into your course folder. Record the prompt as `variation-A-prompt.txt`. Instructions to preserve details are requests, not a guarantee.

## Create variation B

Use the same full prompt as A, replacing only its final finish sentence with the matte ceramic instruction. Keep the original BytePlus logo reference and all other generation settings unchanged:

```text
Turn the supplied BytePlus logo into a glossy 3D badge on a clean white studio background with soft shadows. Preserve the exact BytePlus symbol, the word BytePlus, and their proportions. Use BytePlus blue (#0066FC) for the symbol and dark charcoal lettering. Mount the full logo on one rounded white badge, centered with generous empty space. Front-facing product photography, soft diffused lighting, crisp edges. No other text, no extra logos, no decorative props. Give the badge a matte ceramic finish.
```

Change Save Image's `filename_prefix` to `Course/variation-B`. Press **Run**, then copy its file from `output/Course` into your course folder. Record `variation-B-prompt.txt`. Changing this filename only labels the output; keep the model's generation settings unchanged.

Open the two saved image files in separate image-viewer windows and place them side by side. Compare at the same zoom, then zoom in on the product shape and label. Match the filename beginning `variation-A` or `variation-B` to its prompt.

<Shot src="tutorials/byteplus-badge-b.webp" alt="BytePlus badge finish variation B with a matte ceramic surface, blue symbol and charcoal wordmark on white" caption="Variation B requests matte ceramic; check for unwanted differences as well as the intended finish." />

In these samples, A is a rounded-square badge while B is circular. That shape change was not requested. Record it as an unwanted difference if your brief requires the same badge shape; do not describe these results as a finish-only controlled experiment.

## Review and select

Check each variation against the same questions:

- Are the product's shape, colour and important label details still recognizable?
- Does the visible finish meet the enamel or ceramic request while keeping the composition suitable for your brief?
- Are there unwanted objects, distorted details or altered text?

Select the stronger image and explain your choice using visible evidence. If neither meets the brief, revise one prompt instruction and review another result.

This comparison changes one prompt instruction, not every source of variation. Do not attribute all visible differences to the finish sentence or claim a controlled deterministic experiment.

## Practice

Use the finish requirement from your existing brief for this comparison. With your own product, adapt the preservation instructions but still use its original reference for all three images. Keep the base, both variations, their full prompts and a short selection note.

For the guided BytePlus video, review the glossy base's symbol and typography, then copy `byteplus-badge-base.png` as `selected-image.png`. Your A/B finish choice remains in the selection note; it does not replace the video reference. For an independent product brief, keep your chosen reviewed PNG instead. Do not change an extension to convert a file.

Restore the selected image's prompt in Seedream, then use **Graph > Export** to keep `product-image-workflow.json` with it. Restoring the prompt for export does not require another generation. Keep the original reference separately from the JSON.

## Completion check

- You changed one prompt requirement between the two variations.
- You can explain which image better meets the brief and why.
- You noted unwanted changes to product details or text.
- Your selected image and saved workflow are ready for the video lesson.
