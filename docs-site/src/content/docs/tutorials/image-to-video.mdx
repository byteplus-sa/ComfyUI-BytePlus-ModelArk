**Module 5. Your goal:** Turn the selected image from Module 4 into a short video. Review a **480p draft** before rendering its **1080p final** with Seedance 2.5.

## Before you start

- Complete [Module 4](../refine-product-images/) and keep the selected image.
- Confirm access to Seedance 2.5 in the region of your saved ModelArk key.
- Use the **Image to Draft to 1080p** template for this lesson.

<TemplateFacts file="Image to Draft to 1080p.json" />

## Understand the two stages

**Stage 1:** Load Image supplies the reference to Seedance 2.5 Draft. The first Save Video node saves the 480p preview.

**Stage 2:** Draft to Final receives the draft's `draft_task_id` and produces the 1080p video. The second Save Video node saves the final.

A **bypassed** node is skipped. The template starts with both final-stage nodes bypassed, so you can review the draft first.

<Shot src="templates/image-to-draft-to-1080p-workflow.webp" alt="Load Image connected to Seedance Draft and Save Video, with both final-stage nodes bypassed" caption="Run the draft first, then enable both final-stage nodes." />

## Prepare your shot

<Steps>

1. Click **Download workflow** above, save the JSON, and drag it onto the canvas. This opens the course's **Image to Draft to 1080p** graph.
2. In **Load Image**, press **choose file to upload** and select the generated image you kept in Module 4, not the original product photo. Check its preview.
3. Keep **Seedance 2.5 Draft**, **480p**, **16:9** and **5 seconds** selected.
4. Keep every **control after generate** row on `fixed`. Leave **Draft to Final** and its **Save Video** node bypassed.
5. Edit the draft prompt. Refer to your reference as **Image 1**, then describe the motion and camera movement you want.

</Steps>

<Shot src="tutorials/reference-upload.webp" alt="Load Image node with its choose file to upload control identified for selecting a reference file" caption="The control is the same as in the image lesson; select your generated product image here." />

<Shot src="tutorials/final-bypassed.webp" alt="Close-up of the Draft to Final and final Save Video nodes, both tinted purple because they are bypassed" caption="Before running a draft, both final nodes should be bypassed." />

Try this starting prompt:

```text
Use the product in Image 1 as the subject. Keep the product stationary on the studio surface while the camera makes a slow, smooth push-in. Preserve its appearance and keep the lighting soft. Do not add extra objects or text.
```

The prompt guides the result; inspect product details rather than assuming they are preserved exactly.

## Run and review the 480p draft

Press **Run** and wait for the draft in the first **Save Video** node. Watch the whole clip.

Use the video's play control to watch from beginning to end. The template saves the file in `output/BytePlus/Video`, with a filename beginning `ImageDraft_480p`. Copy it into your course folder before moving on.

- Is the product recognizable throughout?
- Does the camera follow the requested motion?
- Are there visible distortions, unwanted objects or changed text?

If you need another draft, keep both final-stage nodes bypassed. Change the prompt, image or seed deliberately, then run and review again.

## Render the 1080p final

<Steps>

1. Once you choose a draft, keep its image, prompt, settings and fixed seed unchanged. Stay in the same ComfyUI session.
2. Click the header **4. Render reviewed draft at 1080p**. Hold **Shift**, click **5. Save the 1080p final**, then release Shift. Both nodes should have selection outlines.
3. With the canvas focused, press **Ctrl+B** to enable both selected nodes. This is **Control**, not Command, on macOS. Both nodes should lose their bypass tint; the purple background group can remain.
4. Press **Run** again. The unchanged draft is reused from ComfyUI's cache, and its connected `draft_task_id` goes to the final stage.
5. Review the final in the second **Save Video** node. Copy its file from `output/BytePlus/Video`, with a filename beginning `ImageDraft_1080p`, into your course folder.

</Steps>

<Shot src="tutorials/final-enabled.webp" alt="Draft to Final and final Save Video selected together with normal dark node bodies after bypass is turned off" caption="Check both node bodies, not the purple group behind them." />

If your shortcuts are customized, open **Shortcuts** in the sidebar and look up the bypass command. Check the visible node state before running.

The final renders the reviewed draft at 1080p; do not switch the draft node to a regular video model or a new resolution. Before changing the shot again, bypass both final-stage nodes.

<Shot src="templates/image-to-draft-to-1080p-result.webp" alt="Draft and final Save Video nodes showing the same illustrated character example" caption="The template's character example shows the draft and final together." />

<Media kind="video" file="image-to-draft-to-1080p.mp4" caption="Example 1080p final, displayed here as a resized playback preview." />

The **cache** is ComfyUI's temporary memory of an unchanged node's result. Keep ComfyUI running between draft review and final generation. Restarting clears that memory; reopening a JSON does not restore it. The [draft-mode reference](../../concepts/draft-mode/) explains rendering a saved draft ID in a separate workflow.

In your project note, record the reviewed draft and final filenames, motion prompt and seed. State that you rendered the final in the same session, with the draft inputs unchanged and `draft_task_id` connected to Draft to Final. Filenames alone do not establish that two clips belong together.

Recording a draft task ID is optional for later recovery, not a course requirement. The draft-mode reference above covers that separate workflow. Keep any recorded ID private. After completing the shot, bypass both final nodes again before exporting the workflow for reuse.

## Practice

Keep the selected 480p draft, its 1080p final and the workflow JSON together. Use **Graph > Export** for the JSON, following Module 3. Compare product details and motion in the two clips, and note differences from your input image.

Open the final video's file properties or media-player information and confirm **1920 x 1080** for this lesson's 16:9 shot. A filename containing "1080p" is not proof of its dimensions.

## Completion check

- You reviewed a 480p draft before enabling the final stage.
- The saved graph keeps the `draft_task_id` connection, and your note records which reviewed draft you rendered without changing its inputs.
- Both videos are saved and the final is 1080p.
- You can explain the role of each Save Video node and the draft-to-final connection.
