## What it does

Registers a video in your private asset library and outputs its `asset_id`, `group_id` and an `asset://` URI, so a Seedance 2 or 2.5 reference can point at the asset instead of uploading the video each time. It needs Dreamina Seedance Advanced Creation Rights, the ModelArk API key, and the IAM access key and secret key saved in **Settings → BytePlus** (see [API keys](../../get-started/api-keys/)). It generates nothing, and you should only use videos you are authorized to use.

## Inputs

<NodeInputs id="BytePlusCreateVideoAsset" />

### Notes on inputs

- **Video source:** connect `video`, or set `video_url` to a public HTTPS link (mp4 or mov), not both. A connected video is uploaded to Comfy.org storage first (Comfy.org login needed). A link goes straight to the library.
- **Group:** with a `group_id`, the asset joins that group. With an empty one, the node finds the AIGC group whose name is exactly `group_name`, or creates it. Use one group per person.
- **Real people:** verify the person in the ModelArk console, then paste the group ID. The node cannot start that QR-code flow.
- **Waiting:** with `wait_until_active` on, the node waits until the asset is Active. With it off, the asset is still Processing when the IDs come out.

## Outputs

<NodeOutputs id="BytePlusCreateVideoAsset" />

### Notes on outputs

- Connect `asset_id` to an `asset_N` slot of [Seedance 2.5 Reference to Video](../seedance-2-5-reference/). An `asset_N` slot looks up the asset's type with your IAM keys.
- `asset_uri` is `asset://` plus the ID. `info` is the asset as JSON, with its status and type.
- The node also shows `asset_id` and `group_id` as text under the node. Save them to reuse the asset later.

## Limits and tips

- A connected video must be 2 to 30 seconds long, 24 to 60 fps and at most 200 MB when the size is known. Each side must be 300 to 6000 px, the width to height ratio 0.4 to 2.5, and the total pixels 407,696 to 8,295,044. These are checked before any upload; a `video_url` is not checked here.
- The node is not an output node. Connect at least one of its outputs to another node, or it does not run.
- The same connected video, with the same group, project, account and `asset_name`, reuses its earlier asset while that asset still exists. This memory is cleared when ComfyUI restarts. A link in `video_url` is never deduplicated.
- The node waits up to 10 minutes for Active, then stops with an error. The asset may still become Active: list it with [Asset Library](../asset-library/) before you create it again.
- Creating assets is rate limited by your rights tier: 3 requests per minute for Entry, 120 for Advanced and 300 for Premium.
- Creation requests are not retried automatically, so a timeout cannot create a second asset.
- Interrupting a run stops the node's wait. An asset that was already created stays in your library.

## Common errors

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| `[BytePlus] The asset library needs IAM AK/SK with asset-library permission …` | Add the IAM access key and secret key in **Settings → BytePlus**. The account also needs Advanced Creation Rights. |
| `[BytePlus] Asset video must be 2 to 30 seconds long. Current: …s.` | Trim the video to between 2 and 30 seconds. |
| `[BytePlus] Asset video frame rate must be between 24 and 60 FPS. Current: ….` | Convert the video to 24 to 60 fps. |
| `[BytePlus] Uploading the video to Comfy.org storage failed: …` | Log in to Comfy.org in ComfyUI, or pass a public `video_url`. |
| `[BytePlus] Asset … failed processing or review (status …). Check the material and try another image.` | The material was rejected. Check it and try another video. |
