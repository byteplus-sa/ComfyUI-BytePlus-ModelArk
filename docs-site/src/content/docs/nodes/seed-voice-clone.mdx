## What it does

Trains a voice on a clip of someone speaking and outputs its `speaker_id`. You then give that ID to [Seed Speech TTS](../seed-tts/) with the model `seed-icl-2.0`, or use it as a reference source in [Seed Audio 1.0](../seed-audio/).

The node needs your separate Seed Speech API key (Singapore region only) and a voice slot from the Seed Speech console. **This node could not be tested end to end:** in the live test run no voice slot was available, so the steps below describe what the code does, not a verified result.

## Inputs

<NodeInputs id="BytePlusSeedVoiceClone" />

### Notes on inputs

- **`speaker_id`:** a voice slot ID that starts with `S_`, bought in the Seed Speech console, or your own postpaid custom voice ID. A custom ID has 8 to 256 letters, digits, `-` or `_`, starts with a letter and avoids the reserved prefixes and suffixes of Seed Speech.
- **Reference clip:** connect 10 to 15 seconds of clear speech to `audio`, for example from **Load Audio**. The clip is sent as WAV, up to 10 MB.
- **`language`:** the language spoken in the clip. Cloning across languages is not supported.
- **`reference_text`:** optional. If you give the text that is read in the clip, training can fail when the audio differs too much from it.
- **`demo_text`:** optional text of 4 to 80 characters for the demo clip, in the same language.

## Outputs

<NodeOutputs id="BytePlusSeedVoiceClone" />

### Notes on outputs

- `speaker_id` is the voice you trained. Pass it to `custom_speaker_id` of Seed Speech TTS.
- `demo_audio` is the demo clip from the service. If the service returns none, the node outputs half a second of silence and logs a message.
- `status_json` is the last status answer of the service, without the demo link.

## Limits and tips

- Each voice slot can be trained 15 times. Every run with changed inputs trains again and uses one of them.
- The node starts the training, then checks its status every 2 seconds. It stops waiting after 600 seconds and tells you to run the node again later.
- The voice is ready when the status is 2 (success) or 4 (active).
- The first TTS call with a new voice activates the slot and starts its billing.
- Keep `disable_volume_normalization` off unless you want the loudness of the clip kept. Keeping it is meant to give a closer match.
- Interrupting a run stops the node's wait. The training request has already been sent by then.

## Common errors

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| `speaker_id is empty` | Enter a voice slot ID (`S_...`) from the Seed Speech console, or your own postpaid custom voice ID. |
| `is not a valid custom voice ID` | Fix the ID: 8 to 256 letters, digits, `-` or `_`, starting with a letter, without reserved prefixes such as `S_` or suffixes such as `_bigtts`. |
| `This voice slot has no training attempts left` | The slot was trained 15 times. Use another speaker ID. |
| `The reference audio does not match reference_text` | Empty `reference_text`, or type exactly what is said in the clip. |
| `The reference audio is too noisy` | Record or choose a cleaner clip. |
